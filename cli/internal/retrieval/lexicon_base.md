# Base lexicon — Portuguese ↔ English software terms

Ships with gofi and is merged with the project's `.claude/lexicon/sinonimos.md`.
It bridges the language a question is asked in and the language things are
named in: a Portuguese question, English identifiers and headings.

Generic engineering vocabulary only. A term that belongs to one business —
an entity, a product, a partner — goes in the project's lexicon, never here.
Each row is a term and the spellings that mean it; the search walks a row from
either side, never between two spellings.
